    How Pool Chemistry Affects Heater Health in Ontario

    Pool water chemistry is the most common external factor that shortens a pool heater's lifespan across Ontario. Water that runs below a pH of 7.2 is corrosive to copper heat exchangers and will cause pinhole leaks to develop within two to four seasons of exposure. High calcium hardness causes scaling on the heat exchanger walls, reducing heat transfer efficiency and increasing fuel consumption. Chlorine that is consistently above recommended levels accelerates corrosion throughout the heater body.

    During every Swimming Pool Heater Service Ontario visit, our technicians note signs of chemistry-related damage on the heat exchanger and internal components. If early-stage corrosion or scaling is found, we advise on the appropriate corrective steps before the damage progresses. Homeowners who maintain balanced water chemistry and schedule regular heater service see significantly lower lifetime repair costs than those who address each issue only after it causes a breakdown.

    Annual service uncovers problems before they become failures. Here are the most common issues our technicians find during pool heater service visits.

    🔥

    Pool Heater Burner Clogged With Debris

    Over a full pool season, insect nests, leaf debris, and combustion residue accumulate in the burner tray and orifices. A partially blocked burner reduces heat output and creates uneven combustion that stresses the heat exchanger. We clean the burner assembly thoroughly and verify even ignition across all burner ports before completing the service.

    🌡️

    Pool Not Heating as Efficiently as Before

    Calcium and mineral deposits build up on the inner walls of the heat exchanger when pool water has high calcium hardness. This scaling acts as insulation that reduces how much heat transfers from the burner to the water, causing the heater to run longer and consume more fuel. We assess the scaling level and perform appropriate cleaning to restore heat transfer efficiency.

    🔧

    Pool Heater Pilot Assembly Drifted Out of Alignment

    The pilot assembly in a gas pool heater can drift slightly from its optimum position over a season of thermal cycling, making ignition slower and less reliable. A misaligned pilot may still light but will cause repeated ignition retries that wear the ignition board faster. We clean and realign the pilot assembly during every gas heater service visit.

    ⚡

    Pool Heater Safety Switches Tripping at Wrong Point

    Safety switches in pool heaters have calibrated trigger points that can shift slightly over time, causing the heater to shut off at lower flow rates than it should or stay on when it should trip. A drifting switch may not trigger an obvious fault code but will cause intermittent operation problems. We test all safety switches against their specifications during the service.

    🛠️

    Corrosion Starting on Pool Heater Heat Exchanger

    Early-stage corrosion on a pool heater heat exchanger is almost impossible to detect without opening the unit and inspecting the internal components directly. Chemistry-related corrosion that goes unaddressed progresses from surface pitting to hairline leaks within one to two seasons. We inspect the heat exchanger during every service and advise on any corrosion found before it becomes a leak.

    💨

    Pool Heater Venting Partially Obstructed

    Gas pool heater vents can accumulate wasp nests, bird debris, or weather damage over a season of use. A partially blocked vent reduces combustion efficiency and can allow exhaust gases to spill back into the combustion chamber. We inspect the full venting path and clear any obstruction before completing the service.

    📡

    Pool Heater Electrical Contacts Corroding

    The combination of outdoor exposure and pool chemical vapours causes corrosion on electrical terminals, sensor connectors, and control board contacts in pool heaters. Corroded connections cause intermittent faults that are difficult to trace without opening the unit. We inspect all accessible electrical connections during the service and clean or replace corroded terminals.

    📅

    Pool Heater Not Serviced in Over a Year

    A pool heater that has gone more than a year without professional service may have burner fouling, heat exchanger scale, and ignition component wear that compound quietly. The risk of a mid-season breakdown increases with each season skipped. We assess the full unit condition during a catch-up service visit and address what needs correction before the pool season is underway.

    🌱

    Pool Heater Won't Start After Winter

    A heater that shut down normally in the fall but will not restart in spring has often been affected by freeze damage, condensation reaching electrical connections over winter, or a control board that failed during storage. This is one of the most common calls we receive at the start of pool season. We inspect the full system at startup, identify what failed over winter, and get the heater running before the season is underway.
