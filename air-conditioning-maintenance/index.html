    Evaporator and Condenser Coil Cleaning

    Coil condition is one of the most consequential factors in air conditioning efficiency. The evaporator coil, located in the indoor air handler, collects dust and humidity residue over time, reducing both airflow and heat absorption. The condenser coil in the outdoor unit accumulates pollen, cottonwood, and debris that block the airflow needed to reject heat outside. Ontario Service Pros cleans both coil surfaces during every Air Conditioning Maintenance Ontario visit so the heat transfer process works the way it was designed to, supporting dependable cooling performance through every week of the season.

    Refrigerant Pressure and System Balance Checks Around Ontario

    Correct refrigerant charge is essential for efficient, reliable cooling. A system running with low refrigerant pressure cannot absorb heat properly from the indoor air, which causes the compressor to work harder and the home to cool unevenly. Around Ontario, refrigerant issues often develop gradually from small leaks that go undetected without annual pressure testing.

    Ontario Service Pros tests operating pressures and superheat or subcooling readings during every service call to confirm the system is charged correctly. If a refrigerant issue is identified, we explain what was found and what the correction involves before any work is done. Maintaining proper refrigerant balance protects the compressor, which is the most expensive component in any cooling system.

    We've seen every issue a neglected or aging cooling system can develop. Here are the problems homeowners call us about most often.

    ❄️

    AC Running But Not Cooling Effectively

    This is most often caused by dirty evaporator or condenser coils that can no longer transfer heat efficiently, reducing the system's ability to reach the set temperature. Left unaddressed, the compressor works harder than it should and wears out significantly faster than a well-maintained unit. We clean both coil surfaces and test airflow and refrigerant pressure as part of every maintenance visit.

    ⚡

    High Electricity Bills During Cooling Season

    An air conditioner operating with dirty coils, restricted airflow, or a low refrigerant charge uses substantially more electricity to produce the same cooling output as a maintained system. Even a modest efficiency drop adds up over a full summer of daily operation. Annual maintenance restores rated operating efficiency and typically produces a measurable reduction in cooling costs.

    🔊

    Strange Rattling or Buzzing From the Unit

    Rattling, buzzing, or grinding sounds usually point to loose fasteners, a worn capacitor, or a motor that needs lubrication and cleaning. Ignoring these sounds allows the underlying problem to progress until a component fails completely, often mid-season. Our technicians inspect all moving parts, electrical components, and fasteners as part of the standard maintenance inspection.

    💧

    Water Leaking Around the Indoor Unit

    Water pooling near the air handler is almost always caused by a blocked condensate drain line that has filled the drain pan until it overflows. Left uncleared, overflow can cause water damage to ceilings, walls, and flooring. We clear the condensate drain and flush the line during every maintenance visit and confirm drainage is flowing correctly before finishing the job.

    🔩

    AC Turning On and Off Too Frequently

    Short cycling, where the system runs for only a few minutes before shutting off and restarting, commonly points to a dirty coil, refrigerant imbalance, or a failing capacitor that cannot sustain proper startup voltage. Frequent short cycles accelerate compressor wear and raise energy costs at the same time. We test for all three causes and address the source directly, not just the symptom.

    🔧

    AC Worked Last Summer But Won't Start Now

    A system that sat through the off-season without maintenance can develop capacitor degradation, contactor corrosion, or blocked airflow that prevents it from starting reliably. These are predictable failures that an annual pre-season maintenance visit is specifically designed to catch and resolve. We test all startup components and confirm safe operation before the cooling season begins.

    🌬️

    Musty Smell Coming From AC Vents

    A musty or stale odour from the vents when the AC runs is usually caused by biological growth on the evaporator coil or in the drain pan, both of which stay damp during normal operation. This is especially common after a season with no maintenance. We clean the coil and drain assembly and eliminate the source of the odour during the maintenance visit.

    💦

    AC Cooling But House Still Feels Humid

    An air conditioner that cools but leaves the home feeling muggy is often running with low refrigerant charge, causing the evaporator coil to operate at a temperature too warm to condense moisture effectively. A fouled coil can produce the same symptom. We check both refrigerant charge and coil condition during maintenance and correct whichever is affecting dehumidification.

    🏠

    One Room Much Warmer Than the Rest

    When one room consistently stays warmer than others, the issue is usually a blocked or restricted duct run, a partially closed damper, or a dirty filter starving that branch of the system of airflow. Maintenance is the right time to check register balance and duct integrity across the full system. We test airflow at each register and adjust or clear whatever is causing the uneven distribution.
