    What Causes Gas Pool Heaters to Need Repair in Ontario

    Gas pool heaters in Ontario deal with a demanding operating environment. Seasonal cycling from winter shutdown to full summer operation puts mechanical stress on igniters, gas valves, and heat exchanger components. Mineral deposits from the pool water build up inside copper headers and heat exchanger tubes, reducing heat transfer efficiency and eventually causing tube damage if the buildup is not addressed. Outdoor units are also exposed to temperature swings and moisture, which accelerates wear on electrical connectors, pressure switches, and ignition components.

    The most common failure points that bring homeowners to us for Gas Pool Heater Repair Ontario include faulty igniters, worn thermocouples or thermopiles, clogged or failed heat exchangers, failing gas valves, malfunctioning pressure switches, and corroded or water-damaged control boards. Each failure produces recognizable symptoms, and accurate identification of the failure before ordering parts is what separates a repair that lasts from one that comes back.

    Heat Exchanger and Burner Tray Repair Across Ontario

    The heat exchanger is the most expensive component in a gas pool heater and the one most likely to develop problems in older units used across Ontario. It consists of copper tubes that circulate pool water through a heated combustion chamber, and it is subject to both internal scaling from pool chemistry and external corrosion from combustion byproducts.

    When mineral deposits build up inside the heat exchanger tubes, water flow is restricted and heating output drops noticeably. When the tubes themselves develop cracks or pinholes, water can enter the combustion area, triggering the unit's water detection safety shutoff and extinguishing the burner. Ontario Service Pros inspects the heat exchanger as part of every Gas Pool Heater Repair Ontario diagnostic, and for units where descaling or tube replacement is required, we provide a clear assessment of whether the repair cost is justified relative to the heater's age and overall condition.

    Gas pool heaters fail in predictable ways. Here are the problems our clients call us about most often, and what we do to fix them properly.

    🔥

    Pool Heater Lights Then Shuts Off Immediately

    This is almost always caused by a faulty thermocouple or thermopile, the small sensor that confirms the pilot flame is burning and signals the gas valve to stay open. When it weakens with age, it cannot generate enough voltage to hold the valve. We test output and replace the assembly, restoring reliable ignition in a single visit.

    💡

    Pool Heater Won't Ignite at All

    A heater that clicks repeatedly without lighting usually has a cracked, fouled, or incorrectly positioned igniter. The igniter may also fail silently with no click, indicating an electrical fault in the ignition circuit. We test the full ignition sequence, identify whether the igniter, spark module, or wiring is the cause, and replace the correct component.

    ❄️

    Pool Heater Running But Not Heating the Water

    A heater that fires and runs but fails to raise the water temperature is often dealing with a scaling problem in the heat exchanger, a partially blocked burner tray, or a gas pressure issue limiting flame output. Ignoring this wastes fuel and accelerates heat exchanger damage. We inspect the combustion chamber, measure output, and identify the true cause before recommending a repair.

    ⚠️

    Pool Heater Shuts Off Before Reaching Temperature

    Premature shutoff during a heating cycle is usually caused by a failing pressure switch sending a false low-pressure signal, a flow issue reducing water circulation, or a thermostat fault. Each of these causes a different repair path. We test pressure switch function, verify water flow through the unit, and check thermostat calibration to find the actual fault.

    🔧

    Gas Smell Near the Pool Heater

    A gas odour near the pool heater or along the supply line is a safety issue that should not be dismissed. It typically indicates a leak at a fitting, a connector, or the gas valve body. We shut down the gas supply, locate the source with detection equipment, make the repair, and pressure test before restoring service.

    📡

    Error Code on Pool Heater Display

    Modern gas pool heaters display error codes when a sensor, switch, or board detects a fault condition. Codes related to pressure, ignition, water flow, and temperature each point to different systems. We interpret the code accurately, run the relevant component tests, and repair what the diagnosis confirms rather than replacing parts based on the code alone.

    💧

    Water Inside the Pool Heater Burner Area

    Water entering the burner area is most often caused by a cracked or pinholed heat exchanger tube allowing pool water to leak into the combustion chamber. The heater's water sensor shuts the unit down as a safety response. We inspect the heat exchanger thoroughly, assess whether repair or replacement is the right call, and give a clear recommendation before any work is started.

    🔄

    Pool Heater Cycling On and Off Constantly

    Short cycling, where the heater starts, runs briefly, and shuts off before reaching temperature, is typically triggered by low water flow through the heater, a dirty heat exchanger, or a high-limit switch tripping due to localized overheating. Each ignition cycle adds wear to the ignition system and gas valve. We diagnose the water flow rate, clean the heat exchanger if needed, and test the high-limit switch to correct the cycling.

    ❄️

    Pool Heater Won't Start After Winter

    A heater that shut down normally in the fall but will not restart in spring has often been affected by freeze damage to internal components, condensation reaching electrical connections over winter, or a control board that failed during storage. This is one of the most common calls we receive at the start of pool season. We inspect the full system at startup, identify what failed over winter, and get the heater running before the season is underway.
