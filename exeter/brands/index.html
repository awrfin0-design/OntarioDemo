FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Ontario Service Pros provides professional gas service for furnaces, fireplaces, pool heaters, tankless water heaters, and standard water heaters. Our services include inspection, installation, maintenance, repair, emergency gas service, HVAC service, and gas appliance troubleshooting for homes and properties.

You should call Ontario Service Pros for gas furnace repair if your furnace is not heating properly, making unusual noises, turning on and off too often, producing weak airflow, or causing higher energy bills. A gas furnace technician can inspect the system, find the issue, and complete the needed repair safely.

Yes. Ontario Service Pros provides gas furnace installation and replacement for homeowners who need a new, efficient heating system. We help assess your home’s heating needs, recommend the right furnace size, and install the system properly for safe, reliable performance.

A gas furnace should usually be inspected and maintained once a year, ideally before the colder season begins. Regular furnace maintenance from Ontario Service Pros helps improve safety, heating performance, energy efficiency, and the lifespan of your equipment.

Yes. Ontario Service Pros provides gas fireplace inspection, maintenance, repair, and service. If your fireplace will not ignite, has a weak flame, shuts off unexpectedly, smells unusual, or has pilot light problems, a gas fireplace technician can check the system and repair it safely.

Yes. Ontario Service Pros provides emergency gas service for urgent heating, HVAC, furnace, fireplace, water heater, and gas appliance problems. If you smell gas, suspect a leak, or feel unsafe, leave the area immediately and contact your local gas utility or emergency services before requesting repair service.

Yes. Ontario Service Pros repairs gas pool heaters, indoor pool heaters, swimming pool heaters, and pool heater systems that are not heating, leaking, cycling incorrectly, or showing error codes. Professional pool heater repair helps restore safe and consistent water heating.

Yes. Ontario Service Pros provides tankless water heater repair, tankless maintenance, standard water heater repair, and water heater installation. If your water heater has no hot water, inconsistent temperature, low flow, error codes, leaks, or ignition issues, our technicians can inspect the unit and recommend the right solution.
