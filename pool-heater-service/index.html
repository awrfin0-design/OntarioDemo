    Annual service finds these conditions before they cause a breakdown. Here is what our technicians commonly discover during pool heater service visits.

    🔩

    Pool Heater Heat Exchanger Scaling Up

    Mineral deposits from pool water accumulate inside heat exchanger tubes over a season of use, gradually reducing water flow and heat transfer efficiency. The heater compensates by running longer cycles, which increases wear on the burner and triggers high-limit switch sensitivity. During annual service we flush and clean the heat exchanger, restore normal flow, and confirm the system is no longer thermally stressed from restricted water circulation.

    🔥

    Pool Heater Igniter Wearing Toward Failure

    Igniters on gas pool heaters degrade through repeated spark cycles and typically fail without obvious warning during a cold-morning start-up attempt. Catching a worn igniter during a service visit costs far less than an emergency repair call mid-season. We test igniter output against the acceptable range during every gas heater service and replace it proactively when the reading indicates end-of-life is approaching.

    💨

    Pool Heater Gas Pressure Off Range

    Gas pressure at the heater's manifold can drift from the optimal range due to supply-side changes, regulator wear, or seasonal fluctuations. A heater running on incorrect gas pressure burns inefficiently, produces incomplete combustion, and puts extra wear on the valve and burner. We measure manifold pressure during every gas heater service appointment and flag any reading that falls outside the manufacturer's specification.

    ⚠️

    Pool Heater Safety Switch Tripping at Wrong Point

    High-limit switches and pressure switches can drift slightly out of calibration over a season, causing the heater to shut off earlier than it should or continue running when a fault condition is present. Either direction creates a problem: too sensitive and the heater becomes unreliable, too tolerant and it runs when it should not. We test each safety switch against rated specifications during service and correct any unit reading outside the safe operating range.

    🌿

    Heat Pump Evaporator Coil Blocked by Debris

    The evaporator coil on a heat pump pool heater collects leaves, pollen, grass clippings, and dust across a full season. As the coil surface fouls, airflow drops, heat absorption decreases, and the unit compensates by running longer and harder. Annual coil cleaning restores full airflow and keeps the heat pump operating at rated efficiency without forcing the compressor into extended cycles that accelerate wear.

    ❄️

    Heat Pump Pool Heater Losing Refrigerant

    Heat pump pool heaters can develop very slow refrigerant leaks that reduce system capacity gradually rather than suddenly. A unit that was heating well last season may start this one with a charge low enough to noticeably reduce output. During annual service we check refrigerant pressure against the operating baseline, identify any charge reduction, and address the source before it progresses to a point where the compressor is at risk.

    ⚡

    Heat Pump Compressor Starting Unreliably

    The capacitor on a heat pump pool heater degrades over time and eventually reaches a point where it cannot deliver the current needed for reliable compressor start-up. A borderline capacitor causes the compressor to struggle at start-up, draw more current than normal, and occasionally fail to start in cooler morning temperatures. We test capacitor output during heat pump service and replace it when the reading has dropped to a level that indicates failure is likely within the season.

    🏠

    Pool Heater Vent Drawing Incorrectly

    Gas pool heater venting must draw combustion exhaust away from the unit reliably. A partially blocked vent, a vent pipe that has shifted over winter, or a screen that has filled with debris can reduce draw and allow combustion gases to linger inside the heater cabinet. We inspect the venting path on every gas heater service visit, confirm draw is adequate, and clear any obstruction that could affect safe combustion exhaust removal.

    📅

    Pool Heater Not Serviced in Over a Year

    A pool heater that has gone more than a year without professional service may have burner fouling, heat exchanger scale, and ignition component wear that compound quietly. The risk of a mid-season breakdown increases with each season skipped. We assess the full unit condition during a catch-up service visit and address what needs correction before the pool season is underway.
